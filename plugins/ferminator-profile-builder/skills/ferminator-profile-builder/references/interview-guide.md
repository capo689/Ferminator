# Guided onboarding interview

Ask no more than five questions per round. Summarize what was learned before
moving to the next round.

## Round 1 — Identity and materials

1. What name should Ferminator display?
2. Please attach your current resume or equivalent career-history document.
3. Which LinkedIn, portfolio, GitHub, personal-site, or project URLs are yours?
4. May I search publicly for missing professional profiles and show you
   possible matches for confirmation?
5. Is any supplied material private or excluded from the final profile?

## Round 2 — Career evidence gaps

Ask only questions that the source review could not answer:

- Which accomplishments best represent the work you want next?
- What did you personally own versus support?
- What changed because of your work?
- Which metrics are accurate and safe to use?
- Which tools or domains have you used directly?
- Which apparent qualifications should Ferminator explicitly not claim?

## Round 3 — Search direction

- What three role types should Ferminator prioritize?
- What adjacent role would you consider only with unusually strong fit?
- Which titles look appealing but usually describe the wrong work?
- What seniority levels fit?
- Which functions, industries, or work patterns are automatic rejections?

Turn answers into coherent role families. Present labels, tiers, aliases, and
thresholds for confirmation.

## Round 4 — Practical constraints

- Remote, near a ZIP code, either, or anywhere?
- ZIP and radius: 10, 25, 50, or 100 miles?
- Accepted countries, states, cities, hybrid patterns, travel, or relocation?
- Minimum annual base and currency?
- Are jobs without disclosed compensation still allowed?
- Full-time, contract, contract-to-hire, or other?
- Preferred and avoided company types?
- Desired scan cadence and email/dashboard preferences?

## Round 5 — Draft review

Show a compact summary before generating:

- one-sentence search thesis;
- role families with tier and threshold;
- geography and compensation;
- strongest evidence themes;
- exclusion rules;
- factual gaps or conflicts.

Ask the user to correct the model, not merely approve it. Generate only after
material corrections are resolved.

## Follow-up calibration

Initial onboarding cannot perfectly calibrate job scoring. Explain that
Ferminator will learn from Great, Maybe, Wrong, Duplicate, applied,
interviewed, and offer outcomes. Do not fabricate match examples before the
user has reviewed real jobs.
